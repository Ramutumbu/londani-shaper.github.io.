# londani-shaper.github.io.

Hi, I am Londani Ramutumbu.

Welcome to my portfolio!

I am a Civil Engineering graduate who has developed a strong interest in banking, particularly Corporate and Investment Banking and Transactional Banking. I enjoy solving problems, working with numbers and understanding how businesses operate, and I want to build a career where I can use these skills to help businesses make better financial decisions and improve the way they manage their finances.

Seed Project Ideas

Understanding how businesses manage their cash flow
I would like to look at how a medium-to-large business manages its cash coming in and going out, and how banking solutions such as working capital, payments and cash management can help the business operate more efficiently.

Banking solutions for a growing business
I would like to create a simple case study where I take a growing South African business and identify some of its financial challenges, such as cash flow, payments, trade and working capital. I would then suggest banking solutions that could help the business grow.

Skills to Grow

Technical skill – Financial Analysis:
I want to improve my ability to analyse financial statements, understand key financial ratios and use financial information to understand how a business is performing. This is important because CIB involves understanding clients' businesses and their financial needs.

Soft skill – Communication:
I want to become more confident when communicating with clients, colleagues and senior people. In CIB, technical knowledge is important, but being able to explain an idea clearly and build good relationships is just as important.

Over the next 30 days, I want to improve my understanding of CIB and Transactional Banking by learning the basics of products such as cash management, working capital, trade finance and payments. I will also complete at least one simple business case study to practise applying what I learn.
